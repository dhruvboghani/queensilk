Choosing the threads

Every saree starts on a table of spools. Our masters pair body, motif and zari colours, then test them in daylight and wedding lights.

200+ shadesColour-fastReal zari

Set on the loom

Thousands of warp threads go onto the machine. Pick by pick the shuttle carries colour across, and the buta, border and pallu appear line by line.

120+ loomsJacquard8–14 hrs each

The fabric reveal

Six and a half metres come off the loom. Each length is checked by hand for missed picks, loose zari and colour shifts before finishing.

Hand inspectedWashed & finished

The final drape

Before a design goes live we drape it, so you see exactly how the pallu falls. Then it ships to you, or to a shop near you.
